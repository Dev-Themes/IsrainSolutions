# Launch SEO checklist
- Replace placeholder identity, phone, email, address, ABN and licence; confirm all claims.
- Set final domain and check canonical, OG, robots and sitemap in production.
- Complete and verify Google Business Profile; keep NAP consistent across citations.
- Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools; submit sitemap.
- Publish useful service and suburb landing pages before indexing their URLs.
- Obtain genuine customer review permission; do not emit ratings for placeholders.
- Supply privacy/terms and consent-aware analytics policy. Add GA4/GTM only after consent using next/script afterInteractive; no tracking currently runs.
- Configure mail delivery and rate limiting; test successful, failed and spam enquiries.
- Run Rich Results validation and mobile/desktop Lighthouse; inspect keyboard focus and every requested viewport.
