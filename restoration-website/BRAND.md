# Brand direction

The proposed mark combines a roof, water droplet and check to suggest protection and recovery. It is a draft for client approval. Use a clear space of one quarter of the mark width on every side. Minimum mark size is 24 px. Navy is #0B1F3A, blue #1E88E5, aqua #27C2D4 and amber #F59E0B. Headings use a bold sans serif, with a readable sans serif for body copy. The generated concept sheet is in assets-src/logo-concepts.
