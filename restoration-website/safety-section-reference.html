What to do first

Five essential steps
Do not enter standing water near electrical outlets, appliances or wiring. If electricity may be involved, stay clear and contact emergency services or a qualified electrician.
If a plumbing leak is responsible and the shut-off valve is safely accessible from a dry area, turn it off. Do not enter unsafe areas to reach it.
Explain where the water is coming from, when the damage began and which rooms are affected. Ask what you should do while waiting for an assessment.
From a safe, dry location, take photographs of affected areas and visible damage. Keep relevant receipts and notes for your records.
Move small valuables away from the affected area only when you can do so without exposure to electrical, structural or contaminated-water hazards.
Professional assistance can help assess the extent of water damage.
