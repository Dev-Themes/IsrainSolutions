# Reference Notes

## Structure
- Header: Sticky, transparent on top, solid on scroll. Phone CTA visible.
- Hero: Split layout or full width with dark navy background, signature flowing lines.
- Sections: Alternating white (`--paper`) and light blue/gray (`--mist`) backgrounds to create visual rhythm.
- Footer: 4-column layout on dark navy (`--navy-950`).

## Style
- Brand Gradient: Cool blue to navy to heat orange. Used on divider lines and active states.
- Border Radius: Cards use 16px (`xl`), Buttons use 10px (`md`).
- Shadows: Soft, tinted navy shadows to elevate cards on light backgrounds.
- Typography: Display is Saira (italic, bold), body is Inter.
- Animation: Subtle Framer Motion fade up and drift animations.

## Content 
- Home: Hero, Trust Bar, Services Grid, Why Choose Us, Seasonal Split Feature, Steps, Emergency CTA, Reviews, Service Area, FAQ.
- Services: Detailed sections for Heating, Cooling, Refrigeration.
- About: Story, Values, Team, Credentials.
