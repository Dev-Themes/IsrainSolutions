Your resume is your ticket to an interview. Here is how to make it perfect.

## Formatting for Success

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) hate complex graphics. Stick to standard text formats.

### Use Action Verbs

Start every bullet point with a strong action verb like "Orchestrated," "Developed," or "Accelerated."

## Quantify Everything

Numbers stand out on a page. "Increased sales by 15%" is much stronger than "helped increase sales."

## Next Steps

Review your resume today and apply these changes.
