Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

yt-automation is a personal tool used only by its developer to upload videos to the developer's own YouTube channel and to read that channel's analytics.

Data accessed

With the developer's authorization, the app accesses the developer's own YouTube channel data (video uploads, channel information, and analytics) through Google APIs.

This data is used only to upload videos and to analyze the performance of the developer's own videos. It is stored only in private storage controlled by the developer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
