Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

yt-automation ("the app") is a personal, single-user tool operated by Muhammad Shariq ("the developer"). It publishes videos to the developer's own YouTube channel and reads that channel's analytics. Nobody other than the developer uses the app, and the app does not collect data about any other person.

1. Data the app accesses

With the developer's own authorization, through Google OAuth, the app accesses only the developer's own YouTube data:

  • Permission to upload videos to the developer's channel (youtube.upload)
  • Read-only access to the developer's channel and video information, such as video IDs, titles, privacy status and upload processing status (youtube.readonly)
  • Read-only access to the developer's video analytics, such as views, watch time, retention and likes (yt-analytics.readonly)

The app does not read, collect or store viewers' comments or any personal information about viewers.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the developer's videos and to evaluate how the developer's own videos perform, so the developer can decide which topics to make next. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

3. Storage and security

The OAuth credentials (tokens) are stored as encrypted secrets in the developer's private code repository and on the developer's own computer. Video records and aggregated performance numbers are stored in the developer's private repository. Access is limited to the developer.

4. Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, transfer or disclose YouTube data to any third party. Aggregated performance numbers about the developer's own videos (not personal data about any person) may be processed by Google's Gemini API to produce topic suggestions.

5. Retention, revocation and deletion

Credentials are kept only while the app is in use. The developer can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, the stored credentials and any stored YouTube data obtained through them are deleted. To request deletion at any time, email devthemes4@gmail.com; requests are handled within 30 days.

6. Google and YouTube policies

The app uses YouTube API Services. The developer is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and use of Google user data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from children.

8. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking tools.

9. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. Contact: devthemes4@gmail.com
