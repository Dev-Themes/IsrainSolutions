yt-automation

yt-automation is a personal, single-user tool that prepares short videos and publishes them to the developer's own YouTube channel, then reads that channel's performance numbers to plan future videos. It has one user (its developer), it is not offered to the public, and it does not access anyone else's YouTube account.

Developer and channel

Developer: Muhammad Shariq (individual developer)
Channel: Ember & Edge
Contact: devthemes4@gmail.com

What the tool does

  1. Plans a video topic and assembles an edited short video from licensed stock footage and sound, with AI assistance for planning and writing titles and descriptions.
  2. Uploads the finished video to the developer's own channel with a title, description, tags and a privacy setting chosen by the developer in the tool's configuration.
  3. Reads the developer's own video statistics to see which topics perform well.

YouTube API access and why each permission is needed

Permission (scope)Used for
youtube.uploadUploading the developer's videos to the developer's own channel
youtube.readonlyReading the developer's own channel and video information, including the privacy and processing status of an uploaded video
yt-analytics.readonlyReading the developer's own video analytics (views, watch time, retention, likes)
