# PopArt Agency product concept imagery

Created with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. These are speculative concept visuals, not evidence of commercial commissions or manufactured products. Existing concept names, narrative, and PopArt Agency logo artwork are preserved.

Final files: `public/images/products/<slug>.webp` and `<slug>-detail.webp`. Detail images are photographic crops of the matching concept image. Original generated PNGs remain in the image-generation library. No generated file is needed outside the project at runtime.

## Prompt set

Shared direction: one original landscape 3:2 photorealistic luxury catalogue photograph for PopArt Agency's speculative product design portfolio. Center the full product with generous compositional breathing room, believable material textures, contact shadows, crafted lighting, and restrained art direction. No collage, interface, watermark, extra slogans, or competing company/designer logos. Brand lettering must match the exact concept name.

- **noir-edition / NOIR ÉDITION:** luxury smoked-black rectangular glass fragrance bottle, precision brass collar and thick black cap on a dark stone plinth. Small gold NOIR ÉDITION lettering only. Amber caustics and mineral chiaroscuro. Must not resemble Chanel or carry another brand.
- **aura-skin / AURA SKIN:** premium ivory cylindrical skincare pump bottle with matching minimal paper carton, subtle AURA SKIN typography. Muted sage and warm ivory, matte ceramic plinths, soft side light, tactile sustainable packaging.
- **soniq-lab / SONIQ LAB:** sculptural over-ear wireless headphones, matte graphite and brushed aluminium, restrained SONIQ LAB marking. Architectural composition, soft stone grey backdrop, precise rim lighting, premium industrial design.
- **mono-studio / MONO STUDIO:** minimal architectural black leather handbag, sculptural folded silhouette, brushed rectangular clasp, subtle debossed MONO STUDIO only. Warm charcoal stone studio, directional daylight and realistic leather grain.
- **forma-objects / FORMA OBJECTS:** monolithic travertine lounge chair with sculpted ivory upholstered seat. Warm architectural studio, natural side light, strong geometric silhouette and a tiny engraved FORMA OBJECTS maker plaque.
- **tempus-01 / TEMPUS 01:** analogue wristwatch with brushed titanium case, sapphire glass, midnight blue minimalist dial, TEMPUS 01 lettering and neutral dark leather strap. Charcoal studio surface and restrained reflections.
- **vela-light / VELA LIGHT:** sculptural warm ivory ceramic table lamp with softly glowing spherical shade and cylindrical base. Amber illumination, gallery setting and understated VELA LIGHT maker mark.
- **atelier-zero / ATELIER ZERO:** sustainable premium packaging: uncoated kraft sleeve, compressed paper-pulp tray, textured ivory carton with black ATELIER ZERO typography. Tactile still life on warm grey.
- **sora-optics / SORA OPTICS:** black acetate sunglasses with smoky lenses on a chalk-white plinth, subtle SORA OPTICS inner-temple marking, gloss versus matte shadows and minimalist optical photography.
- **kinetic-one / KINETIC ONE:** rectangular smartwatch with brushed aluminium, charcoal silicone band, functional geometric blue face and no existing software logos. Subtle KINETIC ONE engraving and refined grey studio setting.
- **memoire / MÉMOIRE:** textile-bound archival notebook and folded cream stationery, MÉMOIRE silver debossed typography, forest-grey linen texture and warm stone still life.
- **casa-noma / CASA NOMA:** hand-thrown terracotta and chalk-white ceramic vessels, organic architectural profiles, tactile mineral surfaces and small CASA NOMA stamp at the base.

PopArt Agency's supplied transparent and plain logo files are not edited or regenerated.
