# Placeholders Used

The following placeholders are used throughout the site and should be replaced with real data in production:

- `[PHONE]`: Primary contact phone number (e.g., 555-123-4567)
- `[EMAIL]`: Primary contact email (e.g., info@jmcomfort.com)
- `[ADDRESS]`: Physical or mailing address
- `[CITY / SERVICE AREAS]`: The primary service area or a list of cities
- `[HOURS]`: Operating hours (e.g., Mon-Fri 8am-5pm)
- `[LICENSE #]`: HVAC or Business license number
- `[YEARS IN BUSINESS]`: Number of years active
- `[FOUNDER NAMES]`: Name(s) of the founders (e.g., J & M)
- `[TEAM NAMES & ROLES]`: Names and titles for the About page team section
- `[REVIEWS]`: Real customer testimonials
- `[BOOKING_URL]`: URL to external booking/scheduling software (e.g., Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan)
- `[SOCIAL LINKS]`: Links to Facebook, Instagram, etc.
- `[PLAN PRICING]`: Prices for maintenance plans
- `[BRANDS SERVICED]`: Brands the company is authorized or experienced in servicing (e.g., Carrier, Trane, Lennox)
- Image placeholders (in `public/images/placeholders/`)
